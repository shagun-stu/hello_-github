# BBC Website Test Cases

| Test Case ID | Test Scenario | Steps | Expected Result | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TC_BBC_001 | Website accessibility: keyboard navigation | 1. Open the BBC homepage.<br>2. Press the Tab key repeatedly to move through links, buttons and form fields. | Focus moves in a logical order, each focused element shows a visible focus indicator, and all interactive elements can be reached and activated with the keyboard. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_002 | Website accessibility: images and screen reader support | 1. Open the BBC homepage.<br>2. Inspect images and icons, or use a screen reader.<br>3. Check for alternative text and ARIA labels. | Meaningful images have descriptive alt text, decorative images are ignored by assistive technology, and interactive elements have accessible names. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_003 | Homepage loading | 1. Open a browser.<br>2. Enter the BBC homepage URL.<br>3. Wait for the page to finish loading. | The homepage loads without errors, the header, main content and footer are displayed, and images render correctly within an acceptable load time. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_004 | Navigation menu: main menu links | 1. Open the BBC homepage.<br>2. Click each item in the main navigation menu, such as News and Sport.<br>3. Use the browser back button to return. | Each menu item opens its matching section page, the page title and content match the section, and the menu stays visible and usable. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_005 | Navigation menu: responsive menu on mobile | 1. Open the BBC homepage on a mobile device or a narrow browser window.<br>2. Open the menu using the menu icon.<br>3. Select a menu item. | The menu icon is displayed, the menu opens and closes correctly, and the selected item navigates to the correct page. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_006 | Search functionality: valid keyword | 1. Open the BBC homepage.<br>2. Click the search icon.<br>3. Enter a valid keyword, such as "climate".<br>4. Press Enter or click the search button. | A search results page opens with results relevant to the keyword. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_007 | Search functionality: no results | 1. Open the BBC homepage.<br>2. Click the search icon.<br>3. Enter a meaningless string, such as "zzzxxqqq123".<br>4. Submit the search. | A clear "no results found" message is displayed, and the page does not show errors or break. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_008 | Article opening: from homepage | 1. Open the BBC homepage.<br>2. Click a headline or article link.<br>3. Review the article page. | The article opens on its own page, with its headline, content, images and publish date displayed correctly. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_009 | Article opening: back to homepage | 1. Open an article from the homepage.<br>2. Click the browser back button or the BBC logo. | The user returns to the homepage, and the page loads correctly. | Not Executed |
| TC_BBC_010 | Broken links: homepage links | 1. Open the BBC homepage.<br>2. Collect the links in the header, main content and footer.<br>3. Open each link, or check them with a link-checking tool. | No link returns a 404 or other error page, and every link opens the intended destination. | Not Executed |
